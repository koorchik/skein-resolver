SKEIN-R experiment browser

what am I looking at?

Each page replays one experiment of the SKEIN-R paper document by document: the left column lists concept schemes (entity categories), the middle shows the SKOS registry as it grows — concepts with preferred/alternative labels and typed ISO 25964 broader edges (BTG/BTP/BTI) — and the right column shows that document's events: judge verdicts (link / mint / defer), review-pass edges and merges, and the full LLM transcripts.

The strip under each page’s header shows the run’s scored metrics; the ? at its end opens a glossary explaining every score (pairwise vs B³ F1, NIL, R-reach, …).

Use ▶ to play the stream, the slider to scrub, and the legend inside the page for notation. ★ marks the paper's headline run and its remediated configuration. Tick compare to watch two runs side by side — e.g. how two stacks treat the same document.

Every run links to its artifact folder on GitHub (run card, registry, decision log, transcripts). The claims–evidence matrix lives in the repository's docs/CLAIMS.md.
